MobileOffice is a free office app that opens, edits and converts your documents on your phone. No ads, no account, no internet permission: your files never leave your phone.

<b>Documents</b>
• Word (DOCX, DOC, ODT, RTF): type, format, add pictures and tables, convert to PDF.
• Excel (XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV): formulas in Turkish or English, charts, filters, conditional formats, drop-down lists, tables.
• PowerPoint (PPTX, ODP, PPT): present, edit slides and objects, add shapes, pictures and text boxes.
• PDF: search, read aloud, sign, fill forms, mark up, rearrange pages, combine, protect with a password, compress, redact personal data.

<b>Made for Turkey</b>
• UYAP UDF court documents open as UYAP shows them and convert to PDF or DOCX.
• Turkish e-invoices (e-Fatura, e-Arşiv) show in their own design.
• On-device Turkish text recognition (OCR): makes scanned PDFs searchable and turns photos into text.
• Turkish alphabetical sorting, search that ignores the İ/ı difference, ready Turkish templates.

<b>More</b>
• Scan paper and turn pictures into PDF.
• DWG/DXF drawings with layers, measuring and object snaps.
• EPUB e-books, EML/MSG e-mails, VCF contacts, ICS calendars, ZIP and 7z archives.
• Search inside files, auto-recovery, keyboard shortcuts, tablet layout.
• App lock, screenshot blocking, private mode.

Open source; the engines that process documents are written in Rust and run on the phone.
