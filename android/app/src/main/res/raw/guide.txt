# What is BadoOffice?
A free office app that opens, edits and converts Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, UYAP (UDF), e-invoice, drawing (DWG/DXF), e-book, e-mail, contact and calendar files on your phone.
- No ads, no account, no internet permission: your files never leave your phone.
- Made for Turkish: Turkish letters, Turkish formulas (TOPLA, EĞER…), Turkish alphabetical sorting, Turkish text recognition, UYAP and e-invoices.
# Opening and finding files
- Home lists the documents on your phone. Allow "All files access" on first start, or pick a folder.
- The search box matches file names and ignores case and the Turkish İ/ı difference.
- "Search inside files" looks inside documents. Each file's words are kept on the phone after the first read, so later searches are quick.
- Hold a file to start choosing; choose several PDFs to combine them.
- New opens a blank document, spreadsheet or presentation; "From a template…" offers ready Turkish templates such as a petition, CV or budget.
# Word documents
- Tap Edit and type where you tap. If the keyboard closes, tap the page or the ⌨ Keyboard button.
- Hold a word and drag to select; widen the selection with the handles. Bold, colour, alignment, lists, styles and A−/A+ are on the toolbar.
- Insert adds pictures, tables (choose their size) and page breaks. Page setup sets the paper, orientation and margins.
- Save writes over DOCX/ODT/RTF files; DOC and UDF files are saved as a new DOCX.
# Excel spreadsheets
- Tap a cell and type in the formula bar. Numbers use the Turkish form: 1.250,5. While you type a formula, the cells it refers to are framed in colour.
- Tabs: Format (bold, fill, borders, merge, currency), Insert (table, chart, note, drop-down list), Edit (copy, paste), Data (Σ, sort, filter, remove duplicates), Rows and columns.
- Tap a column letter or row number to select it; drag a header's edge to resize.
- Tap a chart to move it, change its type or title, or delete it. PDF turns the sheet into a PDF with its page setup.
# PowerPoint presentations
- Present full screen: tap right for next, left for previous; hold for a laser pointer; speaker notes are shown to the presenter.
- In Edit, tap an object: drag a corner to resize (text grows with it), a side to widen, the top handle to rotate, the object to move.
- The object bar has font, size, colour, alignment, fill, duplicate, bring forward/back and move to another slide. Add shapes, text boxes and pictures.
- ODP and old PPT presentations open too.
# PDF
- Find, Read aloud and Text view work in Turkish. Hold and drag to select text; highlight, underline or strike it out.
- Sign: draw your signature and tap the page. Write & draw: draw, highlight, or type text anywhere on the page.
- Fill form, Pages (rotate, delete, move, add PDF), Redact (ID, IBAN, phone), Password and Compress are in the menu.
- Recognise text reads the page (scanned pages too) on the phone and turns it into text. Make searchable reads every page of a scanned PDF so it can be searched and copied.
# Scanning and pictures to PDF
- Scan photographs paper; the corners are found for you, drag them if needed. The document look removes shadows.
- Pictures to PDF puts several photos in order into one PDF. Combine joins several PDFs into one.
- In the picture viewer, Recognise text turns the Turkish text in a photo into text.
# Other files
- UDF documents open as UYAP shows them; e-invoices show in their own design.
- Measure DWG and DXF drawings: hold and slide to snap to ends, midpoints and centres. Save the whole drawing or the area on screen as PDF.
- EPUB e-books, EML/MSG e-mails (with attachments), VCF contacts and ICS calendars open as documents.
- Files inside ZIP and 7z archives open directly.
# Keyboard and tablet
- Ctrl+S save, Ctrl+F find, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo, Ctrl+P print, Page Up / Page Down turn pages.
- On a wide screen the file list shows two columns.
# Safety and privacy
- The document you edit is backed up every few seconds; if the app closes, you are offered the copy when you open it again.
- Settings can lock the app, block screenshots, turn on private mode and clear recent files.
- The name in My details signs the notes you add. Measurements keep speed and stutter records on the phone until you send them.
